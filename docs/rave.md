# The rave missions: sources and notes

Two linked night missions (src/event-rave.js, src/rave.js; data in src/event-places.js `RAVE`, baked by assets/missions/snap-places.mjs).

## The real place

**Centro sociale ExKarcere**, Via San Basilio 17, 90133 Palermo, in the Albergheria / La Loggia quarter, in a municipal building (the centre is known for the evictions it fought and for its after-school and neighbourhood activities).
- Address: questoapp.com/places-to-visit/palermo/centro-sociale-ex-karcere ("Via S. Basilio, 17, 90133 Palermo PA"), and the local press (livesicilia.it/centro-sociale-exkarcere-sospeso-lo-sgombero, which puts it on Via San Basilio, in the Albergheria, and says the building belongs to the Comune; balarm.it events for the centre, "Centro Sociale ExKarcere").
- Position: OpenStreetMap Nominatim, "Via San Basilio, La Loggia, I Circoscrizione, Palermo, 90133": 38.11887, 13.36104 (the middle of the street; the house number is not on the map). The game's tiles have the same street (Via San Basilio, 6 m wide, from about (-93, -380) to (50, -305) in local metres); the mission's door is the nearest drivable point of it, (-19.4, -348.4).
- The game says nothing about the centre beyond its name; the sound man is invented.

## The game

- **Casse pû rave** (kind `rave`, night): the sound man (a DJ sprite) at the Karcere; carry the speakers up to the rave. 9.4 km by road (baked route: across the city to the foot of Monte Pellegrino, up Via Pietro Bonanno through its hairpins, along the forest service road Via Padre Ennio Pintacuda, and the last 20 m up the track onto the dance floor), time = length / 9 + 55 s (about 18:25; the same formula as for the old 4.5 km site, 9:15), fragile cargo: a sudden loss of speed above 4 m/s costs 3.4 percent of the speakers per m/s (at most 34 percent a knock, 0.7 s apart); at 0 the rave is off, with the clock out too. Pay: 12 + length / 500 euros (31) times the health left, plus up to 8 euros for time left. On delivery the rave is **powered** for the night: louder, brighter, 22 people instead of 12.
- **Fuogghi allegri** (kind `leaf`, night): a friend at the rave sends you to Via Cipressi (11.8 km, back down the mountain and across the city, by the one-way streets the right way), seven leaves lie within 150 m of its end (a green leaf with a small smile: a wink, nothing more), then you hand them to a pal on the street. Time to get there length / 9 + 60 s (about 22:50), 85 s for the hunt, 70 s for the hand-over. Pay 6 + 3.5 per leaf, +10 for all of them. The friend (the caller) really rides along: they walk to the lapa and sit in the passenger seat the fares use (`seatFn`, the clock starts when they are in, or at once if the lapa pulls away), comment on the way (chatter, a scrape, a pedestrian, a hard knock, high speed, each leaf), stay on through the hunt, and get off with the pal at the hand-over. If the mission fails (the clock, nothing found, the leash) they get out and walk off; quitting to the menu or any end of the event removes them (`stop()` in src/event-rave.js).
- **The rave** (src/rave.js, src/rave-ground.js): a round dance floor, 15 m in radius, on a bench of Monte Pellegrino 295 m up, in the forest of the tiles (local (-472, -4504), 38.15647, 13.35587), reached by a 6 m gravel track that leaves the forest service road Via Padre Ennio Pintacuda (a branch of Via Pietro Bonanno, the road up to the Santuario) 38 m away. Stage with two speaker stacks and a DJ on the far side of the floor, six searchlight beams and two floor discs that follow the beat, a ring of lamps, the crowd, and a house loop made of oscillators (kick, clap, hat, bass line at 138 bpm: nothing recorded); the car radio ducks while it is audible. The slab and the track are there day and night, the rest at night only; built within 560 m, the sound is heard within about 360 m and follows the music volume option.
  - The floor is drivable: the ground inside it is level (src/world.js passes the terrain through `raveGround`, like the stadium's pitch; the natural ground is within 1.2 m of the floor's level out to 20 m, then it eases back to the mountain over 11 m), it and the track count as raised ground (the lapa stands a kerb's height over the forest floor, drawn over it) and as hard ground and gravel for the tyres (`raveSurf`), so the lapa goes up the track and round the floor at normal speed, and off the far side into the woods (the pines are not solid). Only the stage is solid (four wall segments, in play while it is drawn).
  - The track is a strip draped on the ground when it is built, and it is built when the camera comes within 560 m, which is before the tiles of the mountain have streamed in (the coarse far grid can be far from the real ground there): src/rave.js compares the ground under the track with what it was built on and draws the strip again when it changes.
  - The dancers are not solid either: a lapa coming close makes them step away, a moving one that touches them sends them flying (a short throw, spinning) and they walk back a moment later.
  - The pines of the woods (src/world.js `buildTrees`, instanced and not solid) and the solid trees of src/props.js keep clear of the floor and the track (`raveClears`, src/tree-clear.js); the pines are not solid, so the track is cleared too. No wall of the tiles is in the way (there is nothing to cut, unlike the Favorita site, which needed a gap in the park wall of Viale Ercole), and the floor straddles the borders of three tiles (z = -4500 and x = -500), which is fine because every one of these is a function of x and z only.
  - The wood round the clearing: the planting of `buildTrees` is a pine in about every 14 m cell with a 0.72 chance, which left only 7 trees in the ring from 17 to 35 m round the floor and none in some directions (a meadow, not a wood). `raveWood` (src/rave-ground.js) adds a second, finer planting (a point in every 7 m cell, the same forest-area and clear-ground tests) from 19 m to 46 m out, fading away by 64 m, only in the tiles within 64 m of the site: 43 trees in the ring from 17 to 35 m now, and 11 to 15 in each eighth of the circle out to 50 m.
  - Why here (the owner wanted it up in the mountain woods, not at the foot of the mountain, and accepted a longer drive).     The whole of the mountain is forest in the tiles, so "the woods" is not the limit; the ground is. A scan of the forest within 25 to 160 m of a drivable road found no ground with under 5 m of relief in 20 m on the mountain below 8.6 km of road (the only gentle spots before that are the Favorita and the lower end of Viale Diana, which are the plain). The chosen bench is the first ground on the way up that a floor can take without any wall: the ground is within 1.2 m of its mean over the whole 40 m disc, the track climbs 2 m in 38 m, and it is dense forest all round with a service road beside it, so no track has to be cut up a slope. The bench just north of it is as good but 170 m further along the service road, with a wall and a house 25 to 60 m from it. The Santuario plateau is the flattest wood (0.6 m) and is 3.5 km of road further, which is 6.5 more minutes on the clock.
- **The leash** (src/missions.js): both have their own area (the line from the start to the destination), with a radius that is the farthest the baked route strays from that line plus 300 m, never less than 650 and 700 m (`raveLeash`, the same rule as the fares). The mountain road swings 1,043 m out of the line from the Karcere to the rave (the first hairpins are far to the east), and the road back to Via Cipressi 1,259 m, so the old fixed radii would have ended the mission on the way up: the radii are now 1,343 and 1,559 m.
- The baked roads (assets/missions/snap-places.mjs): the way up is the shortest road through the drivable network plus the service road, with one-way streets ignored (the street at the Karcere is one-way against its nearest node; a test bot drove it from end to end twice). The first bake of the way back ignored them too, and sent a bot down Corso Alberto Amedeo (Piazza Vittorio Emanuele Orlando), a one-way tunnel in the tiles, where the lapa stopped against a wall at the bottom of the cutting and stayed there until the clock ran out (probably the wrong end of the tunnel; not checked further). The way back now obeys one-way streets (11.8 km instead of 11.1).
- Map pins: violet (rave) and green (leaf), only at night; both stand on the dance floor (the place of the missions is on its axis, 7 m short of the middle, `SNAP['rave:site']`), and the speakers are delivered within 12 m of it.

## Lines for the owner to correct (Palermitan, written by an agent)

All in assets/missions/lines.js (`CALL.rave`, `CALL.leaf`, `RAVE_LINES`) and in the `T(...)` calls of src/event-rave.js: the callers, the thanks, the "Crac!" on a knock, "Casse rutte!", "Taiddu assai!", "Attruova i fuogghi allegri!", "Portali ô cumpari". Spelling after the owner's: stressed o to uo/ua, stressed e to ie, r before a consonant to i.

Changed when the rave moved from the Favorita to the mountain (both are the old wording from before the Favorita, put back, not new Palermitan): the sound man's first line ("Puitali ’n cima ô Pellegrinu, ma araciu, ca sunnu dilicati!") and the result card title ("Rave ô Pellegrinu", "The rave on Pellegrino"); the mission description in src/missions.js says the same ("Carica i casse e puitali ’n cima ô Pellegrinu, ma araciu, ca sunnu dilicati."). The place's name in the HUD is "Rave sul Pellegrino" (src/event-places.js).
