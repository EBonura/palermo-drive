# The rave missions: sources and notes

Two linked night missions (src/event-rave.js, src/rave.js; data in src/event-places.js `RAVE`, baked by assets/missions/snap-places.mjs).

## The real place

**Centro sociale ExKarcere**, Via San Basilio 17, 90133 Palermo, in the Albergheria / La Loggia quarter, in a municipal building (the centre is known for the evictions it fought and for its after-school and neighbourhood activities).
- Address: questoapp.com/places-to-visit/palermo/centro-sociale-ex-karcere ("Via S. Basilio, 17, 90133 Palermo PA"), and the local press (livesicilia.it/centro-sociale-exkarcere-sospeso-lo-sgombero, which puts it on Via San Basilio, in the Albergheria, and says the building belongs to the Comune; balarm.it events for the centre, "Centro Sociale ExKarcere").
- Position: OpenStreetMap Nominatim, "Via San Basilio, La Loggia, I Circoscrizione, Palermo, 90133": 38.11887, 13.36104 (the middle of the street; the house number is not on the map). The game's tiles have the same street (Via San Basilio, 6 m wide, from about (-93, -380) to (50, -305) in local metres); the mission's door is the nearest drivable point of it, (-19.4, -348.4).
- The game says nothing about the centre beyond its name; the sound man is invented.

## The game

- **Casse pû rave** (kind `rave`, night): the sound man (a DJ sprite) at the Karcere; carry the speakers to the rave. 4.5 km by road (baked route, the last 40 m of it up the track onto the dance floor), time = length / 9 + 55 s (about 9:15), fragile cargo: a sudden loss of speed above 4 m/s costs 3.4 percent of the speakers per m/s (at most 34 percent a knock, 0.7 s apart); at 0 the rave is off, with the clock out too. Pay: 12 + length / 500 euros times the health left, plus up to 8 euros for time left. On delivery the rave is **powered** for the night: louder, brighter, 22 people instead of 12.
- **Fuogghi allegri** (kind `leaf`, night): a friend at the rave sends you to Via Cipressi (5.8 km), seven leaves lie within 150 m of its end (a green leaf with a small smile: a wink, nothing more), then you hand them to a pal on the street. Time to get there length / 9 + 60 s, 85 s for the hunt, 70 s for the hand-over. Pay 6 + 3.5 per leaf, +10 for all of them. The friend (the caller) really rides along: they walk to the lapa and sit in the passenger seat the fares use (`seatFn`, the clock starts when they are in, or at once if the lapa pulls away), comment on the way (chatter, a scrape, a pedestrian, a hard knock, high speed, each leaf), stay on through the hunt, and get off with the pal at the hand-over. If the mission fails (the clock, nothing found, the leash) they get out and walk off; quitting to the menu or any end of the event removes them (`stop()` in src/event-rave.js).
- **The rave** (src/rave.js, src/rave-ground.js): a round dance floor, 15 m in radius, in a clearing of the Favorita's pines between Viale Ercole and Viale Diana (local (-1101, -3941), 38.15137, 13.34868), reached by a 6 m gravel track that leaves Viale Ercole 58 m away, through a gap in the park wall. Stage with two speaker stacks and a DJ on the far side of the floor, six searchlight beams and two floor discs that follow the beat, a ring of lamps, the crowd, and a house loop made of oscillators (kick, clap, hat, bass line at 138 bpm: nothing recorded); the car radio ducks while it is audible. The slab and the track are there day and night, the rest at night only; built within 560 m, the sound is heard within about 360 m and follows the music volume option.
  - The floor is drivable: the ground inside it is level (src/world.js passes the terrain through `raveGround`, like the stadium's pitch), it and the track count as raised ground (the lapa stands a kerb's height over the park's grass, drawn over it) and as hard ground and gravel for the tyres (`raveSurf`), so the lapa goes up the track and round the floor at normal speed. Only the stage is solid (four wall segments, in play while it is drawn).
  - The dancers are not solid either: a lapa coming close makes them step away, a moving one that touches them sends them flying (a short throw, spinning) and they walk back a moment later.
  - The pines of the woods (src/world.js `buildTrees`, instanced and not solid) and the solid trees of src/props.js keep clear of the floor and the track (`raveClears`, src/tree-clear.js); the pines are not solid, so the track is cleared too.
  - Why here: the tile tree data (forest areas, which src/world.js fills with a pine about every 14 m) puts the dense woods on Monte Pellegrino itself on slopes of 18 m and more of relief in 16 m, which cannot take a floor without walls, and the gentle woods on the Via Pietro Bonanno side are 8 km of road from the Karcere (Via Pietro Bonanno near (40, -4320), 7.4 m of relief). The wedge of the Favorita between Viale Ercole and Viale Diana is forest in the tiles, level to within 0.4 m over the floor and the track, with about 20 pines between 17 and 40 m of the floor, and is 4.5 km from the Karcere, on the way to Via Pietro Bonanno.
- **The leash** (src/missions.js): both have their own area (the line from the start to the destination, 650 and 700 m round it).
- Map pins: violet (rave) and green (leaf), only at night; both stand on the dance floor (the place of the missions is on its axis, 7 m short of the middle, `SNAP['rave:site']`), and the speakers are delivered within 12 m of it.

## Lines for the owner to correct (Palermitan, written by an agent)

All in assets/missions/lines.js (`CALL.rave`, `CALL.leaf`, `RAVE_LINES`) and in the `T(...)` calls of src/event-rave.js: the callers, the thanks, the "Crac!" on a knock, "Casse rutte!", "Taiddu assai!", "Attruova i fuogghi allegri!", "Portali ô cumpari". Spelling after the owner's: stressed o to uo/ua, stressed e to ie, r before a consonant to i.
