# Faithfulness survey, zone round: the north-west outskirts

Date: 4 November 2026. Game commit shot: c96e15d (a `git archive` snapshot served on its own port). One reviewer, one pass, no game changes. Photos were looked at and live outside the repo; nothing here is copied from a photo. Pictures that show the top problems are in `/private/tmp/claude-501/zone-nw/pick/` (not published).

Zone: Sferracavallo, Tommaso Natale, Partanna, ZEN, Cardillo, Pallavicino, San Lorenzo, Isola delle Femmine, Capaci, Torretta. Place names below come from my own knowledge and are approximate; the street names are from the game's road data.

## What was compared

- **41 new viewpoints, each with a Mapillary photo, spread west to east.** I indexed every Mapillary image in the zone (22,507 images, in 400 m cells over everything with a mapped road). 17,541 of them lay within 15 m of a mapped drivable road and faced along it (within 40 degrees); I dropped panoramas and anything in Mondello proper, then picked by stratum (fast road, ordinary road, each crossed with town centre, town, edge, open country), newest photo first, at least 300 m apart. Round 3's viewpoints were not reused. The list, with game metres and the public image id (not the photo), is in `zone-north-west-viewpoints.json`.
- **Photo ages.** Capture years: 2024 (8), 2022 (10), 2021 (7), 2019 (14), 2017 (1), 2016 (1). Median 5.4 years, range 2.0 to 10.5. Eight are under 3 years old, 17 are 3 to 6, 14 are 6 to 8 (mostly one autumn 2019 drive), 2 are over 8 (nw25, nw41).
- **Usable photo: 38 of 41.** Not comparable: nw01 (the position is 360 m beyond the west edge of the baked map, x below -11058, so the game has no ground there at all), nw24 (the photo is a dark silhouette of a viaduct and the game camera stands inside a wall), nw27 (sun in the lens, only the left and right edges can be read). Partly read because of glare: nw26, nw28, nw38, nw40.
- **Same camera as round 3**: at the photo's position and heading, 1.6 m up, 60 degree vertical fov, 11:00, UI and car hidden, 1024 by 768, 4.5 s settle after the world reported itself calm. 82 shots, all of them looked at: the 41 pairs, 13 repeat shots snapped to the nearest road centre line (below), 12 game-only shots, 16 shots of the same frames with `?nostreetwalls`. 0 unsettled, 0 page errors.
- **Snapped repeats (nwNNs).** Mapillary positions are 5 to 12 m off. For the 13 viewpoints whose photo position was more than 6 m from any mapped road (nw08, 13, 14, 15, 19, 21, 25, 26, 28, 29, 30, 31, 36) I shot a second frame on the road centre line, same heading. This changed the verdict a lot: nw13, nw15 and nw36 looked far worse from the raw position (camera on bare ground or in a wall) than from the road. Findings below say which frame I mean; where I do not, they hold for both. One trap: nw26 snapped onto a 6 m residential lane beside the expressway, not onto the carriageway the photo was taken on; nw29 and nw30 snapped onto the 8 m `tertiary` carriageway itself, which is the one the game draws as a city street (see problem 3).
- **No Mapillary photo exists for the middle of the zone.** There are zero images within 500 m of ZEN, Cardillo, Pallavicino and San Lorenzo, and none within 500 m of Torretta (the nearest mapped road to my Torretta point is 610 m away). I shot 12 game-only frames there (gzn_0 to 3, gcd_0 and 1, gpl_0 and 1, gsl_0 and 1, gto_0 and 1) and judged them from general knowledge of how these places look, not from a photo. Capaci has 371 images from one drive in 2019 and the dense town centres of Partanna and Sferracavallo are covered. Everything between ZEN and Pallavicino is not.
- Sheets read: 4 photo-only sheets (to check the photos before shooting), 7 pair sheets (6 pairs each), 3 snapped-repeat sheets, 2 game-only sheets, 2 `?nostreetwalls` triptych sheets; plus 5 full-size frames (nw04, nw09, nw13, nw15 and the nw13s pick). Sheets are 384 by 288 per picture, so signs, fence types and small furniture are weak evidence; I say so where it matters.

Caveats as in earlier rounds: photos are 2 to 10 years old, phone compass headings are sometimes off, sprites stand in front of the lens, and a different lane of a divided road looks different. "Sure" means a clear difference at thumbnail size, "fairly sure" a likely one.

Mountain shapes are right (nw09, nw13, nw15, nw21). Guard rails exist on the A29 and the Via delle Industrie ramps (nw09, nw12, nw13s, nw32). The monument at nw09 is in the game (two stelae where the photo shows one obelisk). The game's worst frames are not in the dense quarters but in the strip where a town meets the sea or a main road.

## What is systematically wrong in the zone, ranked by how visible it is to a player

Sizes: small is a tuning change or a runtime rule, medium is a few days of one lane, large is new models or a data pass. "Seen" is what I saw in frames; "guess" is a cause I did not prove. File names come from reading the source, not from a change.

1. **Plain tan walls, 2 m tall, along the outskirts roads, with the same torn-patch decal.** Viewpoints: nw02, nw06, nw07, nw08, nw14, nw16, nw19, nw21, nw22, nw30, nw31, nw35, nw37, nw24 (the last two from the other rule below). Seen: a continuous wall at the carriageway edge where the photos show a low parapet over the sea, a green mesh fence, or open ground; it cuts off the sea view at nw22 and nw19. Seen with `?nostreetwalls`: in nw02, nw06, nw07, nw08s, nw16, nw19s, nw22, nw30s, nw37 and nw20 the wall is still there, so it is a mapped wall, not the roadside-wall rule; at nw24 and nw21s the wall turns into mesh when the rule is off, so there the new rule made it. Cause (read, not tested): `tools/fences.py` turns every CTR "wall" line near a carriageway into kind `w` with a made-up height of 1.8 to 2.6 m (its own docstring says the CTR gives no height), so every low sea parapet and field wall becomes a 2 m plaster wall. Size: small as a runtime rule (cap the height of `w` pieces that stand within a few metres of the sea, or beside a verge, in `src/fences.js`), large if the real heights are to come from data. The roadside-wall rule in `src/fence-walls.js` also needs a check for walls in front of a viaduct (nw24).
2. **Bare ochre ground beside the road, with nothing on it.** Viewpoints: nw02, nw05, nw06, nw07, nw08, nw09, nw10, nw11, nw12, nw13, nw14, nw15, nw31, nw33. Seen: pale sandy strips and plains with dark oval blotches, from kerb height to a hundred metres out, where the photos have an asphalt hard shoulder, scrub, hedges, painted parking bays and kerbs. In nw02, nw06 and nw30 the street layer `verge` is under the camera; at nw13, nw14, nw05, nw08, nw10 no street layer or land-cover polygon is under the camera, so the colour comes from the terrain itself. Guess: the terrain base colours and detail texture in `src/world.js` (`terrainColor`, the `plain` and `sand` entries of `C`), the orthophoto ground tint (`tintFn`), `TEX.verge` in `src/streets.js` for the verge strips, possibly the yard mix; I did not isolate which. Size: small to medium (a palette and texture pass, plus scrub or hedge props where the verge is wide).
3. **Fast roads are ordinary roads, and some are city streets.** Viewpoints: nw26, nw29, nw30 (Viale Regione Siciliana Nord Ovest), nw12, nw14, nw15, nw32, nw09. Seen: the Viale Regione Siciliana Nord Ovest frames show zebra crossings, slab pavements, parked cars and bushes, where the photos show a double steel rail, edge lines, tall lamps, a gantry. The A29 ramps and Via delle Industrie have one thin rail or none, no edge lines, a centre dash. Cause (read in the data and `src/expressway.js`): these carriageways are tagged `tertiary` with `ms` 50 in the tiles (checked in the tile around nw29), and `isExpressway` only accepts motorway, trunk and primary with `ms` 70 or more, so they get none of the rail rule and all of the city rules (parked cars from `CAR_CLASSES` in `src/props.js`, pavement polygons, crossings). Size: medium (a named or one-way dual-carriageway override in `isExpressway`, and a matching exclusion from parked cars and crossings; edge lines for the lesser roads are a second step in `src/streets.js`). A map rebake would be cleaner but is not needed.
4. **Cliffs and mountains have the wrong colour and a wavy texture.** Viewpoints: nw09, nw11, nw12, nw13, nw14, nw15, nw21; nw07 and nw19 to a lesser degree. Seen: shape and scale are right, the surface is pale grey and cream with soft wave bands and green patches, where the photos show golden to pink-ochre rock with vertical streaks, scrub and pines at the foot. Cause (partly read): the limestone palette in `src/world.js` (`rock` `#c6c0b2`, `rockLo` `#aaa598`) and the rock shader; guess that the shader's banding reads as waves. Size: small. This is the thing a driver on the A29 and the coast road sees for several minutes.
5. **Street trees and verge planting missing.** Viewpoints: nw04, nw25, nw34, nw38, nw39, nw40, nw11, nw21, nw35, nw37, nw02. Seen: photos have plane, ficus and stone-pine canopies, cypress rows, olive trees and thick hedges; the game has a few isolated low-poly trees and a lawn strip. Read: street trees come from OSM tree nodes and `tree_row` ways, plus hand-picked rules for the old avenues (`src/props.js`); the baked tree props within 80 m of nw04, nw02 and nw36 number 0 and a handful at nw25, nw38, nw40, so the outskirts have no source. Size: medium (plant by street class and quarter in `src/props.js`, as is already done for the Libertà avenues).
6. **Buildings and squares missing at town edges; a lone slab block here and there.** Viewpoints: nw03, nw05, nw13, nw33, nw41, nw10. Seen: a stone square and a Liberty palazzo (nw03), the low houses beside the street (nw33) and the near buildings of Isola (nw05) are not there, while a single 7-storey slab stands alone on the plain at nw13. Not checked whether those buildings exist in OSM; round 3 put this in the same place (`tools/footprints.py`). Size: large (a data pass; needs a map rebake).
7. **Roads too wide and too empty in the towns, drawn with the same centre dash everywhere.** Viewpoints: nw04, nw33, nw34, nw35, nw37, nw18, nw13, nw28, nw41. Seen: a secondary road is 10 m wide in the data (`w` 10) and shows as an open asphalt lot at nw33 where the photo street is narrow; a centre dash is painted on one-lane lanes (nw35, nw37, nw18) and the photo of nw37 is unpaved. The wear patches are large equal squares in a grid (nw04, nw32, nw13s). Size: small to medium (no centre line on narrow or unnamed residential roads in `src/streets.js`; irregular patch shapes in `src/roadwear.js`).
8. **Seafront detail missing.** Viewpoints: nw19, nw22, nw07, nw08. Seen: the rusty railing, the painted bike lane, the rocks and the parapet that give Barcarello and Isola their look are a wall and a beach. Size: medium (props and one road-paint type).
9. **Car craters.** nw09 (two hexagonal pits as large as a car roof), nw10, nw19s (smaller). Size: small (`src/roadwear.js`). Already on round 3's list and still there, fewer than before.
10. **Parked rows and street clutter.** nw34, nw39, nw25, nw17: no bumper-to-bumper cars, no bins, no overhead cables. The boxy car model reads as a box at nw30s and nw28s. Size: small to medium.

Not in the top ten: sprites standing in the road at nw02, nw04, nw06, nw08, nw19 (a game choice, but they do hide the road in the middle of the frame), and the repeated torn-plaster decal on every wall (nw02, nw07, nw08, nw15, nw19, nw22, nw37), which would be less obvious if the walls were lower.

## Re-running this round

Index and select the photos with the Mapillary API (cells of 400 m, token as a request header only, symlink `data/mapillary_token` from the main checkout and remove the link afterwards), write `ref/<id>.jpg` and `meta/<id>.json` in the round 3 shape (`lat`, `lon`, `heading`, `captured`, `age_years`, and `x`,`z` for repeats), `git archive HEAD` into a folder, serve it, run `tools/faith_shoot.mjs --settle 4500` through `/private/tmp/claude-501/withbrowser.sh`, then `tools/faith_sheet.py` and `--grid`. For the nostreetwalls frames I used a copy of the shoot script with `&nostreetwalls` added to the page URL (the committed script has no switch for it). `zone-north-west-viewpoints.json` has everything needed to fetch the same photos again. Photos, shots and sheets live in `/private/tmp/claude-501/zone-nw/`, outside the repo.
