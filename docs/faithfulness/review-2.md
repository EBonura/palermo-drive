# Faithfulness review 2: street-level walk across the whole city

Owner: board item c7 (review only, nothing was fixed; the list is meant to be split between agents).
Method: 30 fixed viewpoints, each shot at street level (camera 2.2 m above the ground, 1280 x 720, frozen at 14:00, `?debug&clean&time=14`) in four headings, so 120 frames. Monuments were shot from 40 to 100 m away from the four clearest sides (chosen automatically by raycast so the camera is not inside a wall), the quarters from the spot itself looking N, E, S, W. Each spot was compared with Wikimedia Commons photos (proportions and what is there, nothing copied). Reference thumbnails are in `/private/tmp/claude-501/palermo-npc/c7/ref` and are not in the repo.
Shots: one contact sheet per spot (our own frames, top row N and E, bottom row S and W) in `/private/tmp/claude-501/palermo-npc/c7/<id>.jpg`, full-size frames in `c7/<id>-<N|E|S|W>.png`, and side-by-side sheets with the references in `c7/cmp-<id>.jpg`. All of it lives in scratch, none in the repo; the `<id>` names below are the spot ids (for example `06-ballaro`).

Caveats, read these first:
- The player car sits 4 to 5 m behind the camera, so a few frames have a traffic Ape or car filling a corner. That is the traffic, not the method, except where noted.
- Commons search gave poor references for the ZEN, Piazza dei Leoni, Stazione Centrale, Zisa, Palazzo dei Normanni (interiors only) and Mondello (collages). For those the comparison rests on what the buildings are known to look like, and each finding says how sure it is.
- Spot 13 (La Cala) was shot from the water by mistake, so the quay side is not judged. Spot 28 (stadium) ended up inside the bowl, so the exterior from Viale del Fante is not judged either.
- I did not find the Pellegrino road itself (spot 24 became Castello Utveggio and the rock face under it, spot 25 the Santuario). A road shot is still open.
