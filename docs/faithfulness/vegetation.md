## Tree species by area (board item n4, 2026-11-02)

Before: every tree without an OSM species (44,000 of 44,216 baked trees) got a random model from one global mix (orange 32, tree 30, ficus 18...), the woods and big parks were one dark conical spruce, and the Libertà planes were the generic broadleaf with a tint.

- **`src/species.js`** (new, pure functions, deterministic per position). Order of decision: (1) an explicit species or genus (OSM `species`/`genus` tags, the monumental register of docs/sources/street-furniture.md: Ficus, Phoenix, Washingtonia, Platanus, Citrus, Olea, Ceratonia, Nerium, Quercus, Pinus); (2) the crown kind: OSM `leaf_type` (needleleaved, palm, broadleaved) and the CTR hint `kd` (tall = palm-like crown, olive, pine, fruit = orchard symbol); (3) a weighted mix by place (named gardens, the Foro Italico strip, the district of `districts.js` `zoneAt`, the height above the sea, the land use around), limited to the crown sizes that fit (a 1.5 m crown is citrus, olive or oleander, a 6 m crown ficus, plane, pine or carob). Mixes: Via della Libertà plane 48 %, Viale Regione Siciliana and the wide avenues plane and oleander, the old avenues plane and ficus, the Foro Italico Canary and fan palms, ficus and plane, the villa gardens and botanical gardens ficus, palms, plane, the Favorita stone pine 26, Aleppo pine 20, holm oak 22, Mondello stone pine, palms, citrus, oleander, the hills (above ~80 m outside the parks) Aleppo pine 38, olive 24, carob 18, holm oak 10, the Conca d'Oro (low land south of 1.5 km and west of -3.2 km in the rural and periphery zones, or any OSM or CTR orchard) citrus. The weights are design values read off aerials and street photos, not measurements; the tables are at the top of the file.
- **Models** (`src/treegeo.js`, shared by the props and the forest instancing): new plane tree (pale mottled trunk, big yellowish-green crown), Aleppo pine (leaning grey-red trunk, thin irregular light olive crown), stone pine (umbrella, darker). Carob, holm oak and olive are the generic broadleaf with a tint, citrus the orange tree, oleander the flowering shrub. The palm models are now two fan palms (a, b) and one date palm (b, the tall one) instead of three and two, to pay for the new ones.
- **Woods**: the forest instancing of `world.js` (OSM forest areas, and parks larger than 400 m across: the Favorita) plants Aleppo pines and, in the Favorita and at Mondello, stone pines (40 to 50 %): two instanced meshes per tile at most. Town parks no longer get the wood: their trees are placed by `props.js` by species.
- **Groves**: the CTR fruit orchards (1,525 polygons, 19 km2, mostly citrus in the Conca d'Oro) and olive groves (242) are baked as land cover (`tools/tiles.py`, `orchard` and `olive` areas; what lies within 8 m of an OSM building is cut away, the survey is from 2003): the ground reads as a grove and `props.js` plants one tree per ~100 m2 up to 300 a tile. A CTR orchard symbol (`kd: fruit`, one mark for a few trees) stands for a short row of four. 357 tiles gained or changed areas; +0.6 MB of JSON, +0.13 MB packed.
- **Medians**: the narrow green and verge strips beside the named avenues get oleander shrubs (about one in 12 m2) and the odd plane tree.
- **Draw calls** (spot-bench, desktop and phone): within the run-to-run noise of about 5 percent, Favorita 514 to 426 (the spruce wood is gone), Foro Italico 479 to 461, Via della Libertà 492 to 496, Mondello 445 to 480, centro 517 to 550, Conca d'Oro 259 to 280 (groves). Triangles +4 % at Mondello, +30 % at the Conca d'Oro spot (the groves).
- **Not done**: species are not baked into the tiles (they are chosen at build time of a tile's props), so the minimap and the far city boxes do not know them; cypresses and the horse-chestnut/jacaranda street trees are not modelled.
