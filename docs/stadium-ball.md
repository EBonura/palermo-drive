# The Barbera ball

`src/stadium-ball.js`, created by `createStadium` in `src/stadium.js` and updated from there only while the lapa is on the pitch (the same `on` test as the crowd's hype). Away from the pitch nothing runs: the mesh, the shadow and the score card are hidden and the score of the visit is dropped.

- One sphere (radius 1.5 m, rosanero and black panels), one shadow disc, no allocation per frame. Physics in 10 ms substeps: gravity, bounce (0.62), rolling friction, side boards and end lines of the pitch rectangle (the pitch's own frame, from `obb` of the OSM playing area, like the goals in `src/lmc-a.js`), the four goal posts as small circles.
- The lapa is two circles along its body; a hit gives the ball the relative speed along the contact normal plus a share of the lapa's own speed and a small lift.
- The goal frames of `src/lmc-a.js` block the lapa only; the ball ignores them and goes through the mouth into the net. GOL = ball past the goal line (52.5 m from the centre), between the posts (3.7 m) and under the bar (2.44 m).
- On GOL: the banner, the stands held at full hype for 7 s, a chant balloon from a seat in front of the camera, +10 points (+5 per goal inside 45 s of the last, up to +25), a EUR 2 credit through `pd-fare` at most once every 20 s, the best score saved in `localStorage` (`palermoDrive.stadium.best.v1`), the ball respawns at the centre after about 3 s. Event `pd-stadium-goal` `{ n, points, add }`; the secret achievement `primo-gol` listens to it.
- Test hook with `?debug`: `window.__ball` (`state`, `set(x, y, z, vx, vy, vz)`).

Palermitan lines to be checked by the owner: the achievement title "Primu gol ô Barbera" and note "Facisti gol cû lapa ô Barbera.", the GOL chants ("GOOOOL!", "Gol! Gol! Gol!", "Alé alé rosanero!", "FORZA PALERMO!"), the "punti / serie" words on the banner (Italian; English when the game is in English).

## The stands, the sound and La partita

`src/stadium.js` also runs the reactions of the Barbera while the lapa is on the pitch (the same `on` test):

- **Balloons from the stands.** A fan balloon roughly every half second, plus bursts for what the lapa does (comes in, drives fast, hits the ball, spins, parks, a goal, a shot wide, over the bar or on the post). Lines in `src/stadium-lines.js` (`[Palermitan, English]` pairs, drafts in `docs/palermitan-review.md`). They are `shout(..., { crowd: true })` balloons: at most 8 at once, their own cap and element pool in `src/shouts.js`, smaller and without the blur, so the street's balloons are never pushed out. Seats are picked in front of the camera, away from the lapa, the minimap and the cards at the top, and not on top of another balloon.
- **Sound.** `src/stadium-audio.js`, all synthesised (noise beds, a sung vowel for the "oooh" and the boos, a big drum and claps in a chant rhythm, air horns, the referee's whistle, the crowd's catcalls), built on the car radio's AudioContext (`radio.context`, which exists after the game's first tap), so nothing starts before the first gesture. Volume follows Options volume (`radio.sfxVolume`) and fades with the distance from the pitch (full on it, about a fifth at 300 m, gone beyond about 450 m, duller with distance). Goal: roar, horns, whistle at the restart. Near miss, post, wide, over the bar: a gasp and a groan (whistling when wide). Tension as the ball nears a goal: the bed rises, a vowel climbs, the drum quickens. No node is made per frame; the graph is made when the lapa comes within hearing and thrown away five seconds after it has left.
- **La partita.** While the lapa is inside, the radio card is taken over (`radio.takeover`, theme `match`, `lock: true` so the next-track key does nothing): label "La partita", the score as the title (`PALERMU 1 - 0 OSPITI`, a goal at the +u end is Palermo's) and a commentary line (`retext`). The player's station is ducked to silence (`duckFor(k, 1)`) and comes back when the lapa has left. The card's own sound is the same crowd through a small speaker (band-limited, overdriven), plus the whistle and a short jingle on a synth tone, at the music volume (and silent when the radio is off). Not selectable as a station.
- Test hooks with `?debug`: `window.__stadium` (`audio.debug()` with the audio graph state and RMS levels, `off = true` to switch the new reactions off for a before/after, `cost()` the milliseconds spent in `update`, `score`).
- No spoken commentary: nothing is recorded or faked. A text-to-speech voice could read the commentary lines, but browsers have no Palermitan voice, so it would sound like a tourist; left out for the owner to decide.
