# The horns mission, "U Sinnacu a primavera"

A Palermo joke made into a mission, `src/event-horns.js`, day or night. There is a real saying that nobody passes under the arch of Cutò without scratching their head (the horns); the mission and its lines rest on that saying and point at nobody.

- **Start**: a citizen (a pedestrian variant picked among the older and the shopper types) waits at Via Maqueda next to Piazza Pretoria (the fare spot `pp` is the same square). Stop next to them like for any caller. Map pin colour `#ffb02e` (`COLORS.horns` in src/event-calls.js).
- **The horns**: one merged geometry (two tapering curved horns, a leather plate, a gold ring), about 3.6 m wide, placed on the vehicle's roof from the vehicle's own seat height (`vehicle.seats[0].y + 1.0`, `+ 0.8` on bikes), so it fits every vehicle. They wobble: two damped springs (about 1.3 Hz) pushed by the lapa's acceleration. They pop on with a springy scale-in when the citizen hands them over (4.4 s) and come off whenever the event ends (done, failed, leash, quit).
- **The arch**: the gate is in the middle of the model's passage (`CUTO_PASSAGE` of src/lmc-cuto.js: middle 272.3, 530.5, heading into Ballarò `thIn`, clear width 3.9 m); if the model is missing the baked road point `SNAP['horns:arch']` is the fallback. The horns are about 4.3 m across, so the tips reach 0.2 m into the jambs; the sparks come from the jamb lines. Baked fallback: `SNAP['horns:arch']` (x, z and the direction of travel towards Ballarò: `src/event-routes.js`, baked by `assets/missions/snap-places.mjs` from `HORNS` in src/event-places.js). The trigger is a gate across the street there: crossing it from the Maqueda side (inside 4.6 m of the centreline, moving towards Ballarò, after having been on the Maqueda side within 80 m) counts. Coming from Ballarò shows "Di ddà un vali!" and does not count. It does not depend on the arch model.
- **At the arch**: sparks from the horn tips, a synthesised screech (Web Audio, no recording), the lapa loses speed, lines about scratching your head, the people waiting beyond the arch laugh (a synthesised laugh and balloons). Speed at the gate: under 4 m/s is "too careful" (a long screech, a joke line, 7 EUR), over 13 m/s is "too careless" (the horns break off in sparks, 7 EUR), in between is clean (10 EUR + up to 6 for the time left). Clock: path length / 5 + 90 s. Secret achievement `arcu-cuto` through the `pd-horns` event.
- **Route**: Via Maqueda is a pedestrian way in the tiles, not in the drivable network, so the baker builds its own network with Via Maqueda added for this route only (`loadNet({ extra })` in assets/missions/roadnet.mjs); the other baked routes are untouched.
- **Leash**: 330 m around the segment from the square to the arch.
- **Lines**: `HORNS_LINES` in assets/missions/lines.js and the `T(...)` calls of src/event-horns.js, all listed in docs/palermitan-review.md.
