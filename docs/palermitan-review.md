# Palermitan conversion of agent-written lines

Plain Italian lines written by agents, converted to Palermitan with the owner's spelling (stressed o to uo/ua, stressed e to ie, r before a consonant to i; truappu, zuana, piaiddiri). Old on the left, new on the right; English subtitles are unchanged. Please correct whatever is not how it is said. Not touched: the owner's own lines (src/shouts.js, the driver and vendor lines), the tourists' lines, the Italian words of the UI.

## The Barbera crowd and La partita (src/stadium-lines.js)

Drafts written with the owner's spelling; please correct what is not how it is said. English is the subtitle shown when the game is in English. The score line on the card reads "PALERMU 0 - 0 OSPITI" ("PALERMO 0 - 0 GUESTS" in English): a goal at one end counts for Palermo, at the other end for the guests.

## Two more night clients: Via Lincoln and Via Bandiera (assets/missions/lines.js, NIGHT_CLIENTS)

Same system as the client of A Favorita: a man who winks ("vabbé u sai") and wants not to be seen. The joke is on his embarrassment only: nothing explicit, nobody at the destination is described or mocked. In Via Bandiera he goes "nni Nicoletta a Trimmutura" (the owner's wording: a famous woman of nineteenth-century Palermo, gone for over a century); she stays offstage, nothing about her is described, and the gag is the client who insists she is still receiving. Nothing here says anything about her beyond the nickname the owner supplied. Drafts, English subtitles ours.
