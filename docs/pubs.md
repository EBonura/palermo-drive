# Palermo pubs and bars: builder reference

Research notes for the night-time pub spots (people outside in the evening, one night-only mission each). Compiled 1 Nov 2026.

All reference photos are stored outside the repo in `/private/tmp/claude-501/palermo-pubs/refs/<venue>/`. They are third-party images (venue pages, news sites, review sites, Wikimedia Commons), kept for looking only. Per-venue contact sheets are in `/private/tmp/claude-501/palermo-pubs/refs/_contact_sheets/`. Thumbnails were found through image search, so the original page is named only where the search result showed it.

## How sure each fact is

- Coordinates come from OpenStreetMap (Nominatim and Overpass). An OSM node is a hand-placed point and can sit 10 to 20 m off the real door. Treat lat/lon as "on the right stretch of street", then use the photos for the facade.
- Mapillary was checked for every venue with the project token. It has almost no coverage inside these pedestrian lanes (nothing within 40 m of any venue, and only a few 2014 frames beyond 130 m near Krust and Whisky & Drink), so no Mapillary photos are used.
- Sizes are my estimates from photos and OSM building footprints, not measurements. A storey in these buildings is about 3.5 to 4.5 m. Hex colours are eyeballed from photos, and night colours are strongly tinted by sodium street lamps.
- "Open now" was judged from activity dated Aug to Oct 2026 in search results and directory listings. I could not call anyone. Where I have only older evidence I say so.

## General look of the streets (for all venues)

- The Kalsa/Vucciria/Capo lanes are narrow, paved in grey-brown stone slabs (basolato, about #8A7F72 wet at night), with 4 to 6 storey ochre and cream stucco buildings, iron balconies, and many shuttered ground floors covered in stickers and graffiti.
- Via Lattarini's street sign is a black plate with white serif capitals and carries Latin, Hebrew and Arabic text (Wikimedia Commons `Palermo - Via Lattarini.jpg`, saved at `refs/commons_lattarini/via_lattarini.jpg`). A nice detail for the street signs in game.
- Night light is warm sodium orange (#E0A050 on walls) plus string lights and shop-front glow. Piazza Sant'Anna at night (`refs/commons_piazza_sant_anna/piazza_santanna.jpg`) is mostly dark stone with white spotlights on the church side and a few lamps.
- The Politeama area (Via Dante, Via Carducci, Piazzetta Bagnasco) is different: 1890s to 1930s Liberty and eclectic apartment blocks, 5 to 6 storeys, wide pavements, ficus trees, green or brown wooden shutters, majolica lozenge patterns on some facades (`refs/commons_via_dante/via_dante.jpg`).

## 1. Krust (owner list, main easter egg)

**Real name:** Krust Pub (also styled "Krust, Music and Drink"). Instagram bio calls it "Official Page Krust pub Via dante 19 palermo Metal". Not to be confused with a Facebook fan page "Krust meme pub Dante".

**Location**
- Address: Via Dante 19, 90141 Palermo (piatti.menu listing and the venue's own Facebook and Instagram text seen in search results). The street door photo shows number 21 on the wall next to the door, so the entrance sits between 19 and 21.
- Lat/lon: 38.1243791, 13.3528857, OSM node 5410425438 (`amenity=bar`, `name=Krust`, no address tag, Nominatim places it on Via Dante).
- "Beginning of Via Dante": Via Dante runs from the Via Libertà/Piazza Castelnuovo end near the Politeama toward the southwest. Numbers rise away from that end (Dante Cafe, the former Kilt, is number 39 at the corner of Via Villafranca). Number 19 therefore sits close to the Politeama end. That matches the owner's description.
- Next door: Via Carducci, with Whisky & Drink about 30 m away (section 2). OSM building around the node is a block of roughly 26 x 22 m (way 521748026).
- Sources: Nominatim `search?q=Krust Palermo`, Overpass `way` check for Via Dante (ways 683550692 and 1011293940), https://piatti.menu/restaurants/palermo-1/krust-pub , https://www.balarm.it/news/li-dove-prima-c-era-il-kilt-nel-cuore-di-palermo-la-nuova-vita-di-un-angolo-storico-126828

**Front (from photos, see below)**
- Building: 5 to 6 storey late-19th-century block. Pale cream stucco (#E4D5B8) with horizontal rustication lines on the ground floor, a base course of grey rough-cut stone blocks about 0.8 m high (#8C877F), and a band of geometric green and cream tile frieze above the first storey.
- Entrance: a single recessed bay, about 3.2 m wide and 3.6 m high. Around it is a raised surround in dusty pink stucco (#C99AA0, about 0.3 m wide), like a pilaster frame. Inside the frame is one dark green roller shutter (#2F4A38) about 2.2 m wide and 2.8 m high. There is no shop window. By day the shutter is down and the place looks closed. Night photos show the shutter lifted to a dark doorway with an amber floor.
- Sign: one square plate about 0.8 x 0.8 m, centred above the shutter on the pink surround. White field (#EDEDED) with a thin dark bevel. Red (#B01E23) hand-drawn sun or spiral-gear logo in the top half and "KRUST" below it in black, rough brush-cut capitals. The poster version shows the same logo in red on black camouflage with "KRUST" in white grunge lettering and "MUSIC AND DRINK" in small red serif capitals underneath. The sign is not backlit.
- Fixtures: two black wrought-iron wall lanterns on either side of the door at about 2.4 m, and two split air-conditioning units on the wall beside the sign. Two dark green wheelie bins stand against the wall at the door. A grey cast-iron street lamp post stands on the pavement right in front (it is in the foreground of the facade photo).
- Next door: a pharmacy with a green cross, a shop with a red-lettered sign (Schillaci multi-brand outlet) and a vertical "OUTLET" board. A black cloth hangs from a first-floor balcony above.
- Outdoor tables: none visible in daytime photos. The Aug 2024 brawl report says "chairs and tables flew", so there is some outside seating or crowd space on the pavement at night, but I could not confirm layout. Suggest 3 to 4 high tables and a cluster of standing people on the pavement.
- Interior glimpse for the "easter egg" moment: dark red and black walls, bar with hundreds of bottles, AC/DC and rock band stickers and flags, a Moretti and Ceres tap, a guitar on the wall, red light.

**Character**
- Crowd: metalheads, regulars, rock and punk kids, band members and tattooed locals, age mostly 25 to 45 (photos show bearded men in black band shirts, horns and middle-finger poses behind the bar). Staff are friendly. Piatti reviewers call it a social meeting place more than a pub.
- Drinks: cheap. Piatti's listing gives shots 1 to 2 EUR, spirits about 4 EUR, cocktails about 5 EUR, beer 1.50 to 4 EUR. Menu is Moretti and beer, plus burgers and grilled sausage from a grill.
- Hours: 19:00 to 03:00 every day (piatti.menu listing).
- Music: heavy metal and extreme metal at pub volume, plus metal gig nights. The poster for "Dispepsia Night" is dated 13 Sep 2025 at Krust Pub, Via Dante 19.
- Street scene: the crowd spills onto the pavement with drinks late at night. In Aug 2024 a fight broke out around 2 a.m.
- Palermitano detail: it sits a few steps from the Politeama, in the same cluster as Whisky & Drink. A French metal travel blog page ("Les Antres du Metal part 7") lists it as the metal bar in Sicily. The logo is a sun and gear, instantly recognisable on the metal scene.

**Status:** Open. Facebook page snippet dated 7 Sep 2026 and Restaurant Guru snippet dated 19 Sep 2026 both show it active. History: closed by the questore for 7 days after the Aug 2024 brawl (headline "il questore chiude per 7 giorni il pub Krust", livesicilia.it, not readable here) and reopened; a shutter notice "VACANZA!! riapriremo lunedi 7" is a police closure notice or holiday note on the shutter (photo `ddg_9`). Reports read: https://palermo.gds.it/articoli/cronaca/2024/08/31/palermo-scoppia-una-rissa-al-pub-krust-di-via-dante-volano-sedie-e-tavolini-ferito-un-dipendente-37b5fdb1-0583-4fe6-977a-a7a014cec62f/

## 2. Whisky & Drink (owner wrote "Wisky and drink")

**Real name:** Whisky and Drink, signed "Birreria / Drinkeria Whisky and Drink". Listings also call it "Whisky and Drink Due" (the Carducci shop) and there is a second shop "Whisky and Drink Magione" (section 8). It is a beer and drink pub with a rock theme, not a specialist whisky bar.

**Location**
- Address: Via Giosuè Carducci 38, 90141 Palermo (the venue's own logo reads "Via Carducci 38, Palermo"; Firmania, Cylex and others agree). OSM has it at number 36.
- Lat/lon: 38.1245025, 13.3526089, OSM node 269241275 (`amenity=bar`, `name=Whisky & Drink`, `addr:housenumber=36`).
- It sits 25 to 30 m from Krust, around the corner from Via Dante on Via Carducci (both can share one street scene). OSM building: way 278475397, about 17 x 26 m.
- Sources: Nominatim and Overpass; logo seen in `refs/whisky_drink/ddg_21.jpg`; https://www.blogsicilia.it/palermo/blitz-anti-covid-nella-movida-di-palermo-chiusi-per-assembramento-due-pub/555438/

**Front**
- I found no usable photo of the exterior. What the sources say: in a 2020 rectification published by BlogSicilia the company says its outdoor area is a platform (a raised wooden pedana) with six tables. Via Carducci is lined with apartment blocks; the street edge likely has parked scooters and the pedana at the kerb.
- Sign/logo: white high-contrast serif capitals "WHISKY AND DRINK" on black, with a tribal ornament behind, and "BIRRERIA/DRINKERIA" and the address in small spaced sans capitals underneath (`ddg_21`). The Magione sister logo uses orange and red flame lettering on black (`ddg_15`).
- Interior (`ddg_4`, `ddg_6`, `ddg_11`): warm ochre sponged walls (#D9A441), dark wood shelving with bottles, football scarves hung from the ceiling beam (Holland, Barcelona, Spain), neon red and green light strips behind the bar, a bass guitar on the wall, chalkboards for beer (0.2 l and 0.4 l prices) and cocktails (Moscow Mule, Daiquiri frozen 5 EUR, Mojito 4 EUR), a plain wooden bar with a wooden tap tower.
- For the model, suggest: ground-floor shopfront about 5 m wide with dark double doors, black fascia with the white logo, a low wooden platform with 6 tables and string lights, and a handful of people standing with beers on the pavement.

## 3. Taverna Azzurra (Vucciria)

**Location**
- Address: Discesa dei Maccheronai 9 (some listings say 9/11 or 15), Vucciria, 90133 Palermo.
- Lat/lon: 38.1176395, 13.3634460, OSM node 4622670991 (`amenity=bar`, `addr:housenumber=9`).
- It is in the Vucciria lane just below Piazza Caracciolo (OSM node for Ai Bagnoli at Piazza Caracciolo 11 is about 40 m away). OSM building way 279881608, about 20 x 21 m.
- Sources: Nominatim, Overpass; https://www.tavernazzurra.it/taverna-azzurra-vucciria ; https://www.balarm.it/news/la-taverna-azzurra-torna-alle-origini-e-dice-addio-alla-movida-notturna-cosa-cambia-140145

**Front** (very well documented, photos `ddg_7`, `ddg_8`, `ddg_16`, `ddg_18`, `ddg_23`)
- A single ground-floor frontage about 6 m wide and 4 m high in a faded pale grey-beige stone-look facade (#B8AE9A). Above the shopfront is a long raised sign band about 0.9 m high carrying large carved or painted capitals "MAGGIORE" (the old Maggiore family sign, in serif, grey on cream, letter height about 0.5 m). Under it, in smaller blue-grey (#4C6A86) capitals: "TAVERNA", a small "TA" monogram in a diamond, then "AZZURRA". Door number plate "9" on the right edge.
- Two bays: a left glass shop window with the shelves of bottles inside lit blue, and a right open doorway entered by a few steps down. Both are framed in bright turquoise-blue paint (#1FA0C8). A narrow pilaster between them is covered with stickers and posters. A pull-down shutter in cream sits at the right edge.
- Above the entrance runs a blue canvas awning (#2E6FB7) with a steel-and-wood pergola frame of dark rafters, which reads as a ribbed blue canopy from below.
- Inside, visible from the street: big wooden wine barrels on a rack, shelves full of bottles to the ceiling (the owners call the back shelf "la libreria"), a wood-clad counter with a hatch at the window.
- Street scene (photos `ddg_1`, `ddg_2`, `ddg_3`, `ddg_4`, `ddg_6`): the whole lane is packed shoulder to shoulder at night. Young people hold plastic cups of wine and bottles of beer, there are no tables or chairs, only the odd bench, with plenty of shopfronts down the lane glowing with signs.
- For the game: tight 4 m wide stone lane, pack 30 to 60 people in front, string and shop lights, no road traffic, possibly scooters at the end.

**Character**
- Crowd: university students, young Palermitani, musicians and artists, tourists. Weekend nights are the busiest ("Friday night goes off", Yelp snippet).
- Drinks: Zibibbo, "Sangue di Sicilia" (their sweet red), Marsala, Malvasia, draught beer and bottled beer, wine in plastic cups, pane cunzato (bread with toppings).
- Hours: Mon to Sat 10:00 to 23:00 on its own website (closed Sunday). In Oct 2023 the owners announced closing at 22:00 instead of staying open into the small hours (balarm, 16 Oct 2023). So evening photos of a packed lane are older; today the drinking crowd stays on the lane until closing.
- Music: no band, just talk and speakers from nearby stalls. Occasional guitar on the lane.
- Palermitano detail: "Ci vediamo in Taverna" is the standard meeting phrase. It opened on 17 Oct 1896 as a stable and was later run by the Maggiore family (hence the sign). Salvatore "Zio Totò" Sutera took it over in 1982 and died in May 2021, and his sons Pietro and Michele run it now. The name Azzurra is thought to come from the sky-blue pigment used on the walls. The Vucciria market itself is gone by night but the lane is the scene.

**Status:** Open, with shortened evening hours. Yelp and travel guides list it in Sep 2026. The questore also closed it for 10 days in March 2018 ("ritrovo di pregiudicati"), after which a sit-in reopened it. So any time-of-day logic should treat the peak crowd as before 22:00.

## 4. Mercato San Lorenzo (Sanlorenzo Mercato)

**Real name:** Sanlorenzo Mercato.

**Location**
- Address: Via San Lorenzo 288, 90146 Palermo (San Lorenzo district, in the north of the city, far from the old centre; about 6 km from the Vucciria by straight line).
- Lat/lon: 38.1653202, 13.3176098, OSM node 9201943017 (`amenity=food_court`, name "San Lorenzo Mercato"). Vineria node 10068627646 at 38.16528, 13.31790. Nearby: Mind House pub, Via San Lorenzo 273 (node 13136276279).
- Sources: Nominatim, Overpass, https://www.sanlorenzomercato.it/

**Front and building**
- A converted warehouse or old covered market, industrial: bare stone and brick walls (#C9B79C), exposed steel trusses and corrugated metal roof, long glazed side. The entrance sign on a dark glazed or black fascia reads "SANLORENZO / MERCATO" in thin white serif capitals (photo `ddg_4`, a cyclist blurring past). The wall around the glass is beige stone slabs.
- Inside (`ddg_0`, `ddg_1`, `ddg_3`, `ddg_5`, `ddg_9`, site photos): a long hall with counters on both sides in warm wood and steel. A red awning above "Carnezzeria" (butcher-restaurant) with white text, black and white checkerboard floor in places, hanging lamps and string lights, many small shops (11 "botteghe": bakery, gelateria, pizza, fried food, rosticceria, poké, burger, Vineria wine bar, Baarìa beer bar). Covered terrace with picnic tables, blue and yellow paper bunting and plastic chairs (site photo `site_IMG_3846_VSCO-scaled.jpg`). There is also a garden used for barbecues ("Griglia nel giardino", flyer `ddg_14`).
- Palette from the flyers: red (#D32F2F) with yellow hand lettering, navy night sky banners for "Stelle a Sanlorenzo" live music.
- No lit facade photo found. Suggest a plain beige box about 24 m wide and 8 m high with a long dark glazed front, white serif sign, and warm glow from within, plus a car park and bikes outside.

**Character**
- Crowd: families at lunch, groups of friends and couples in the evening, football fans on match nights. It is an eating and craft beer crowd, not students on the street.
- Drinks: craft beer (the site says about 300 beer mugs a day), Sicilian wine (80 labels), cocktails at the Vineria, cannoli and sfincione.
- Hours: every day. Café 08:00 to 24:00, bakery 10:00 to 24:00, food counters 12:00 to 15:00 and 18:00 to 24:00, Baarìa beer bar 19:00 to 24:00 (evening only).
- Music and events: live music nights ("Stelle a Sanlorenzo", a Vasco Rossi tribute, Thursday 4 Sept live night), match nights (Palermo vs Padova), Oktoberfest 2026, wine fest, sfincione festival, cannolo festival. Night events are routine.
- Distinctive detail: a beer mug is the site's icon (about 300 mugs a day) and the "Il miracolo di Sanlorenzo" campaign uses red wooden-plank graphics.

**Status:** Open. Site has 2026 uploads (Aug 2026) and an "oktoberfest26" banner.

## 5. Lizard

**Real name:** Lizard Pub (Facebook calls it "Lizard Pub & Live Club"). Not "Lizard Nuts" on Sluurpy, which is a mislabelled listing with the same address.

**Location**

## Practical hints for the builder

- Cheapest accurate build order: Krust, Taverna Azzurra, Punkfunk first (best photos, three very different shapes: a plain beige door with a tiny sign, a lettered stone front with blue trim, a black glazed shopfront with a deck).
- Krust: a plain beige-and-pink frame, green shutter, white square sign with a red sun. Almost no outdoor furniture. The easter egg works because it looks unremarkable and is tiny. The crowd stands on the narrow Via Dante pavement.
- Taverna Azzurra: the sign text "MAGGIORE" plus "TAVERNA AZZURRA" is the whole identity. Blue frames, a blue awning and a crowd with no tables.
- Punkfunk: black sign box with white condensed text, a wooden deck with dark tables.
- Lizard and Whisky & Drink Carducci: no exterior photos found. Build from the sign and interior notes, or ask the owner for a photo.
- Sant'Anna cluster (Monkey, Drunks): same piazza, so build the piazza once and add two small fronts.
