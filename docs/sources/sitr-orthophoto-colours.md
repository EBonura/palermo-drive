# Regione Siciliana, SITR orthophotos (ATA 2012-13): roof and ground colours

Used for: `data/roof_colours.json` (a colour and a confidence per baked building) and `data/ground_colours.json` (ground colours per 20 m cell and class). Only colour values derived from the pictures are stored. No imagery is kept in the repo, and the downloaded blocks live in a cache folder outside it.

- Source: Regione Siciliana, Assessorato Regionale del Territorio e dell'Ambiente, Dipartimento Urbanistica, Nodo Regionale S.I.T.R. "Ortofoto Regione Siciliana ATA 2012 2013". Four-band (RGBI) orthophotos from the digital aerial survey of 2012-13, flown with a DMC frame camera, 25 cm pixel (the dataset record puts the positional accuracy at that of 1:10,000 mapping, 4 m).
- Services used (ArcGIS REST `export`, EPSG:25833, the source's own projection):
  - `https://map.sitr.regione.sicilia.it/gis/rest/services/ortofoto/ortofoto_2013_15cm_comuni/MapServer`: 15 cm, the municipalities of Enna, Messina, Palermo, Ragusa and Trapani. 133 sheets (`ATA1213_PAL-*`) cover Palermo, and 78,750 of the 81,941 baked buildings lie under one.
  - `https://map.sitr.regione.sicilia.it/gis/rest/services/ortofoto/ortofoto_2013_25cm_sicilia/MapServer`: 25 cm, the whole region, used for the 3,191 buildings outside the 15 cm sheets and as the colour reference (see below). Its WMS 1.3.0 is listed in the same record.
- Licence as stated in the record (https://www.sitr.regione.sicilia.it/geoportale/it/metadata/details/608, the service record "Ortofoto Regione Siciliana ATA 2012 2013 - Servizio di consultazione", metadata dated 02/05/2022): "Condizioni per l'accesso e l'uso: nessuna condizione applicabile", "Vincolo di accesso: nessuna limitazione", "Vincolo di fruibilità: nessun vincolo". The page footer of the geoportal says "Licenza CC BY 4.0".
- Decision: the owner reviewed that record and decided to use the data with credit.

If it matters, ask area2.sitr@regione.sicilia.it.
- Date of the imagery: flown 2012-13, so about 13 years old. Buildings built, demolished or re-roofed since are not reflected, and the roof colours of a few percent of the city are wrong for that reason. Newer sets exist on the same server (`ortofoto_2019_20cm_sicilia` AGEA 2019 and `ortofoto_2022_20cm_sicilia`, both ImageServer, 20 cm). Their licence is not covered by the record above and has not been reviewed, so they are not used.
- Fetched by `tools/orthophoto_fetch.py` (blocks of 512 m plus 48 m of margin at 0.25 m per pixel, 3 threads, 0.4 s between request starts, 722 blocks, about 1.4 GB). Colours made by `tools/orthophoto_colours.py` (see its docstring for the method). Cache, side-by-sides and logs were kept under `/private/tmp/claude-501/palermo-prep-colours/`, outside the repo.
- What is stored: per building `[hex, confidence, shaded]`; per 20 m cell and class a hex colour and a quality digit; the three-number colour correction in `data/orthophoto_calibration.json`. Nothing else derived from the pictures. The pictures are not copied, thumbnailed or embedded.
- Known issues:
  - The 15 cm export is over-exposed and cold (sun-lit asphalt reads `#96acb8` where the 25 cm set gives `#828a8e`). The colours are therefore scaled per channel (r, g, b gains 0.82, 0.80, 0.76, median over 360,000 neutral pixels in 51 blocks) to the colour balance of the 25 cm set; saturation is not touched. These are picture colours, not measured albedo.
  - Colours are of sun-lit surfaces. Roofs and ground with no sun-lit pixel are lifted by the city-wide lit/shade ratio and flagged (`shaded`, low quality digit).
  - OSM outlines and the photo disagree by up to a few metres, and tall roofs lean in the picture. A per-block offset is applied where the evidence is clear (see `outline_offset_blocks` in the meta), the footprint is shrunk by 0.5 m plus 4 cm per metre of height, and confidence drops for buildings over 12 m.
  - Ground cells exist only in the 512 m blocks that hold at least one baked building (971 of the tiles); hills, farmland and sea without buildings have no entry.
  - Cars, awnings, laundry, solar panels and pergolas are partly filtered (shadow, vegetation and dark-blue panel pixels are dropped, then the dominant colour mode is taken), not all of them.
- Credit line for the README: `Roof and ground colours derived from the Regione Siciliana SITR Ortofoto ATA 2012-2013 (Regione Siciliana, Assessorato Regionale del Territorio e dell'Ambiente, Dipartimento Urbanistica), CC BY 4.0. Colours were extracted and rebalanced; no imagery is included.`
