# MASE PST-A lidar: canopy crowns

Used for: crown tops, radius and height in `data/trees.json` (source `lidar`, `lidar+ctr`) and `data/trees_osm_crowns.json`.

- Licence: CC BY 4.0, the same 2 m DSM (last return) and DTM rasters the game already reads (`tools/lidar_raster.py`, decoded from the WMS colour ramp; nothing new was downloaded). Coverage is the coastal belt only: 32.9 km2 of canopy model (centro, Liberta east of about x = -190, Foro Italico, Mondello, Pellegrino, Favorita south, Brancaccio east). ZEN, Boccadifalco, Zisa, Uditore and the south-west have none.
- Method (`tools/green.py lidar_crowns`): canopy height = DSM minus DTM at 2 m, masked outside building footprints (dilated 2 m), bridges, water; Gaussian smoothing (sigma 1 px); crown tops are local maxima in a 5 x 5 pixel window above 2.5 m (3.5 m above 60 m elevation, 5 m above 100 m, where the decoded ramp is coarse). Radius: eight rays from the top until the canopy falls under a third of the peak (at least 2 m) or rises again, mean clamped to 1.5 to 10 m. Height: maximum in 3 x 3 pixels.
- Rejected: crowns whose surrounding roof is about as high (roof edge leaking out of the mask, 5,652), crowns under 2.2 m radius within 4 m of a facade (2,366), points in the sea (28). Peaks inside OSM forest or scrub are kept and flagged `f:1` (3,768).
- Numbers: 22,929 peaks, 135 matched an OSM tree within 4 m (those give `trees_osm_crowns.json`), 20,339 new trees with a crown. Of the 1,121 OSM trees inside the lidar area, 55 % have 2.5 m or more of canopy within 4 m, so lidar finds roughly half of what OSM maps (young trees, palms with thin crowns, trees close to facades are missed).
- Known issues: DSM is last return, so heights are a low bound for dense crowns; crown radius is a rough estimate (2 m pixels). The lidar date predates the 2018 Urban Atlas by years; trees planted or felled since are not reflected. Tall thin shapes (height over 3.2 times radius, 8 m or more) carry `k:"tall"` as a hint for palms, cypress or poplar. Ships, cranes and containers in the port are not masked.
