# The landfill mission: sources and notes

"Bbaiecca 'a munnizza, e a chi ci sì bbaieccati puru tu" (the owner's title, used exactly as written everywhere). Code: src/event-dump.js (the mission), src/discarica.js (the site as it is drawn, the plunge, the lift out of the pit), src/discarica-ground.js (the ground: the pad, the pit, the track), data in src/event-places.js `DUMP`, baked by assets/missions/snap-places.mjs. In the Missioni list it is in Maluchiffari, with the rave and the horns.

## The real place

**Discarica di Bellolampo**, Località Bellolampo, on the hill above the north-west of Palermo, platform on the S.P. 1 at km 4+900 (Comune di Palermo; the convention paper of the University of Palermo gives the address: internationalstudents.unipa.it/dipartimenti/stebicef/.content/convenzioni_2022/Rep.-31-Protocollo_intesa_Salmeri.pdf).
- Position: OpenStreetMap, `landuse=landfill`, `name="Discarica di Bellolampo"`, two ways, read from the Overpass API (overpass-api.de, 4 Nov 2026, one query for the whole area, no retries needed): **way 1490070878** (59 ha, centroid 38.14421, 13.27361, game x,z -7764, -3150) and **way 320772217** (39 ha, centroid 38.14819, 13.27621, game -7401, -3564). The road is Via Bellolampo (`highway=secondary`, `ref=SP1`, way 666679659) and, past it, the service roads inside the landfill (`highway=service`, ways 40647065 and others). Nominatim was not needed. The quarter Bellolampo is OSM node 2546095117 (38.13725, 13.27321).
- The baked tiles do not draw it (no landfill kind in `areas`, no label), but they have the lidar ground of it: the old landfill is a dome 55 m high between two hills (the near grid reads 469 m in the valley and 525 m on top of it), ringed by the service roads of the tiles, which the game does draw (they have street polygons under them).
- Inside the bake box (docs/TILES.md: 38.045 to 38.230 N, 13.235 to 13.455 E), tiles -17 to -14 by -8 to -5.
- Mapillary: nothing inside the fence (the nearest photos, 2019, are on Via Bellolampo up to 38.1386, 13.2747: a guard-railed road with bags of litter along it and the whole city in the view; looked at, not kept or copied). No aerial photo was needed to place it.
- **What is not real**: the pit. The tiles have no vasca and the sources do not say where the live cells are, so the game cuts its own cell (84 by 72 m at the rim, 18 m deep, three terraces) in the open ground 71 m inside the east edge of way 1490070878 (38.14480, 13.28139; the whole pit and its fence are inside the polygon but for the south-east corner of the fence, 4 m out), next to a bend of a service road that is in the tiles (38.14481, 13.27987, 134 m west of the pit). The landfill is placed to the polygon (tens of metres); the pit within it is an invention on the nearest open, gentle ground (a 0.08 slope falling to the east; the scan that chose it is in the commit message of the site).
- Seen from the road: the stepped terraces, a black pit of rubbish with white and colourful bags, tyres, fridges and mattresses, a yard fence with a gate and a sign, floodlight masts, a bulldozer and a compactor, a column of foul haze, and gulls. Real vasche are lined cells with terraced banks and a perimeter fence and gate, as described by the sources the owner gave.

## The game

- **Where it starts**: a worker (the `tuta` sprite) on Via Bellolampo at the foot of the road up to the landfill (38.13898, 13.27281 snapped to the road: -7746.7, -2570.9, facing the way of the route). Stop next to him like for any caller; or Vai from the Missioni list. Pin colour `#8a6a3a` (brown). Day or night.
- **The way**: 1187 m by the baked line (`ROUTES.dump`, drawn by the pink guide): Via Bellolampo to its end, the service roads round the old landfill, the bend where the track leaves them (the mouth, -7128.4, -3216.0), the track and the pad to a point 6 m short of the rim (-7043, -3215). The roads of the landfill are unnamed service roads in the tiles, so the baker adds them to the network (`loadNet({ extraFn })`); the network the built roads make is cut at two segments of 99 m near (-7200, -1582) (the tiles have no street polygon there), so Bellolampo and the landfill are an island of it, which only matters to a lapa coming up from the city (it drives that stretch on the terrain) and to the baker, which snaps there without asking for the main component (`snap(..., { anyComp: true })`). `tools/route-check` flags nothing on the leg.
- **Clock and pay**: time = length / 9 + 55 s (3:07), like the rave. Pay 10 + length / 500 (12), + up to 6 for the time left, + 3 for a jump (the lapa passes the rim at 8 m/s or more; slower is a roll and pays no jump); the same shape as the other Maluchiffari missions (the horns 10 to 16, the rave 31 and up). Repeat: the slot cools down 150 s after a win, 40 s after a failure (src/missions.js endEvent), as the others do. A win is stored in the Missioni list like any other (`prog.done`).
- **The win**: the lapa is in the pit when the ground under it is 5.5 m or more under the rim (the first berm and deeper): a jump from speed, a drive over the edge at a crawl and a slow roll all count, and so does a lapa that glides in on wings. The mission does not look at how fast: the ball model does (below).
- **The fall**: the ball model of src/vehicle-sphere.js sticks the ball to the ground, so a cliff would just be a steep slope. `hooks.plunge` (one line there, `plunge: null` in src/core.js) lets `plunge()` of src/discarica.js take the ball over the rim: where the ground ahead (one metre, or 0.12 s of speed) is lower than the ball by more than half a metre per metre, and the lapa moves at more than 1.8 m/s, the ball goes into the model's own free fall (`S.air`, the one a vaulted bridge parapet uses) with the speed it has; under 1.8 m/s the slope itself pushes it over (it is steeper than every vehicle's `slopeMax`). While it falls, `car.deck` holds the height over the ground, so the chase camera stays on the body. Every landing from more than 2.2 m up gives a crash (`S.hit`: the shake and the thud of the ball model, a camera punch, a burst of up to 70 bags and bits thrown up from the pool, and dust), and the terraces are taken one after the other when the speed is high.
- **The moment**: the mission sets `vehicle.camMoment(k)`: the chase camera backs off 7 m and rises 4 m, the field of view opens 9 degrees, while the lapa falls and for 1.3 s after it lands on the floor; the driver shouts, the card pays (the result card with the owner's title), and the confetti of the other cards goes off.
- **Out of the pit**: when the card has been shown the screen is covered by the transition (src/transition.js) and the lapa is put on the service road at the mouth of the track facing away from the pit (`recover()`, `SNAP['dump:mouth']`). The same lift takes a lapa out of the pit when it has been in it for 7 s with no mission of this kind running (a drive in on a whim, a failed run). A miss (driving along the rim, stopping at it, a clock that runs out) costs nothing but the clock: the lapa can always back away, and the leash and the clock end the mission like any other. The yard is fenced (solid chain link, a 7.2 m open gate where the track comes in): the crowd of the street does not walk in (src/pedestrians.js reads the yard as a wall).
- **Map**: a brown triangle for the landfill on the minimap, a brown caller pin with a small pit glyph in the start map (kind `dump`), and a start spot "Discarica di Bellolampo (la strada di servizio)" in the start picker.
- **Cost**: nothing is drawn or built until the camera is within 520 m (dropped at 640 m, one build per visit unless the tiles under the track stream in); the haze (56 points in a shader, one draw) is seen from 900 m; the gulls (34 instances, day only) within 260 m and their cries (Web Audio, quiet) within 190 m. Built: 1800 bags (a 36-triangle ball each), 300 boxes, 120 tyres, 40 drums, a bulldozer and a compactor, three masts, the fence, the ground (about 500 vertices): 30 to 50 more draw calls and 50 to 90 thousand more triangles on a desktop screen, and 55 percent of the rubbish on a phone. The ground is a function of x and z in src/world.js (`dumpGround`, one rectangle test everywhere else). Measured with the perf harness of tools/build/spotbench.mjs (main-thread ms per frame, one run each; the phone profile 844 by 390 at DPR 3 with a 4x CPU slowdown is good to about 1.5 ms): far away (Quattro Canti, three runs each, alternating with the same commit without this work) 2.73 ms before, 2.75 after; at the road 700 m from the pit 2.30 (nothing to compare); 400 m from it 2.55 against 2.16; at the gate 2.61; at the rim 2.53 against 2.38; in the pit 2.37 (all 60 fps). On the phone profile: 11.4 at 700 m, 12.3 at 400 m against 11.3, 12.2 at the gate, 11.4 at the rim against 10.5 to 11.5, 11.5 in the pit.

## The ground

The pad is level at 442.2 m (the mean of the baked near grid, 435.6 to 448.4 m, over 14 m of margin round the rim), eased back to the natural ground over 22 m (a cut of up to 6.2 m on the high west side, a fill of up to 6.6 m on the low east side, at the extremes of the pad). The pit is defined on the 5 m nodes of the terrain mesh and read with the same triangles the mesh draws, so what the lapa drives on is what is drawn (`dumpDepth`): risers 6 m high over 5 m (51 degrees), berms 6 m wide, the floor 30 by 18 m at 18 m under the rim, with lumps of 0.5 m. Gravel (surface 1) on the pad and the track, loose rubbish (surface 4: less grip, drag, bumps) in the pit. The overlay mesh over the ground (earth on the risers and berms, a quilt of rubbish on the floor) is draped on the same nodes.

## What was driven (headless Chrome, the built game, scripted keys)

From the caller to the win with the bot of tools/route-check (the whole 1187 m, 122 s at 12 to 14 m/s, 64 s left on the clock), then the jump from 110 m before the rim: the calessino flat out (29 m/s at the rim, 17 m fall in one flight, 1.1 s in the air, card 12 + 5.5 + 3 = 20.5), at 14 m/s (three landings down the terraces), at 3 m/s and at 1.2 m/s (the slope pushes it over, it takes the terraces one after the other), stopping 3 s at the rim and then creeping, reversing over the edge (tascia), diagonally (lapa50), and along the south edge without going in (motorino: the mission goes on, nothing falls). Flat out also with the cassone (heavy), the lapa50, the vespa, the scooterone, the motorino and the tascia, and creeping with the motorino and the cassone: every one lands on the floor (depth 17 to 18 m), gets the card and is put back on the service road. The phone profile (844 by 390, touch controls, 4x CPU slowdown), night (`?time=22.5`), `?lang=en`, `?physics=classic` (no plunge, the lapa drives down the slope and the mission still counts it), `?p2p` on (no error; another player sees the lapa fall through its height over the ground, the pit is the same function of x and z on their side, they see no crash debris, which is drawn locally), a second mission straight after the first through the Missioni route (`ready()`: the prog counts n = 2), the leash (a jump 1.5 km away ends it with the usual card in 20 s), a lapa left in the pit with no mission (back on the road after 7 s, twice). No console error in any run. `tools/route-check/check.mjs --only dump --drive 1`: the line and its three stray lines pass; the drive arrives, with one stop of 2.5 s at (-7491, -2955) where breakable fence panels of the tiles stand within 6 m of the service road (probably what stopped it, not checked further; the same on both drives).

Not driven: a lapa with the wings fitted (it skips the plunge by design and lands in the pit through the air module; the win test is the same), the sulky (a vehicle of the night race only), a real phone.

## Smells found

- The built road network is cut at two segments of Via Bellolampo near (-7200, -1582) to (-7155, -1670): the tiles have no street polygon under 99 m of the road, so `roadnet.mjs` BUILT drops them and everything up the hill (Bellolampo, the landfill) is a separate piece of the network. Not touched here.
- Random pedestrians of the crowd walk on any open ground, including terraces and landfills (they did on the pit until the yard was made a wall for them).
- A world-wide `hooks.plunge`-like rule (the ball leaves the ground when the ground ahead falls away faster than a ballistic arc) would also make real cliffs on the mountain roads fall instead of slide; it is limited to the 100 m round the pit here on purpose.
