# The U cuinnutu fare

A taxi fare, day or night, in the style of the night clients (`NIGHT_CLIENTS.cuinnutu` in assets/missions/lines.js, the fare spot `nc-cuinnutu` in src/event-places.js). A man waits in Piazza Sferracavallo, nervous and saying he is calm, and wants the Hotel Eufemia in Isola delle Femmine, "that is it". It is a nod to a local story and points at nobody: no names, no woman mentioned, nothing explicit. The jokes are on his nerves and on the driver's deadpan.

- **Start**: Piazza Sferracavallo (Nominatim 38.1982, 13.2767), a road point on the square. Map pin colour is the fare pink. It shows as "U cuinnutu" in the Missioni list (group Fares, no night icon) and on the start map.
- **Destination**: Hotel Eufemia, Via Nazionale (SS113), Isola delle Femmine. The hotel has no name in OpenStreetMap. Its place is the unnamed `building=hotel` footprint w1211538241 (tile -20_-18, the only hotel building in the part of the town I looked at), about 20 m from the coordinate the Kayak page of the Hotel Eufemia gives (38.19337, 13.25114) and about 170 m from the railway station (Hotels.com says 3 minutes' walk). The two listings disagree on the house number (Hotels.com 113/30, Kayak 28), and the 38.1803 to 38.1820 stretch of Via Nazionale in the brief is where Nominatim puts "Via Nazionale 113", 1.3 km south in the open country; neither is a survey, so the pin is a reading, not a fact. The drop point is the road point on Via Nazionale in front of the car park lane (-9610, -8576), the passenger walks towards the building.
- **Route**: 3037 m of baked road, Piazza Sferracavallo to Via Nazionale, taken by the guide arrow. Pay is the night client formula (base 9 + length / 70, rounded to 0.50, plus tips, speed tip, and the discretion bonus), clock length / 8.4 + 30 s.
- **Sign**: src/lmc-eufemia.js adds a navy "HOTEL EUFEMIA" board on the wall of that building that faces the road and a blade sign on two posts by the car park lane. Only the name, nothing else about the hotel.
- **Lines**: `NIGHT_CLIENTS.cuinnutu`, all listed in docs/palermitan-review.md. His `ambient` lines come in order (`seq`), `stages` fire `near`, `near2` and `close` at 600, 300 and 110 m from the hotel, `arrive` has the "accumpagnami". `label` and `ped` are the HUD title and the sprite (a daytime suit).
- **Leash**: the usual 520 m corridor around the straight line pickup to hotel is too narrow for this road (it bends 610 m away from the line), so `leashArea` in src/missions.js now widens the radius to the baked route's farthest point plus 300 m for any fare. It affects only the long fares whose road strays more than 220 m from the line.
