# Danger level draft per game area

Draft for the owner to review and adjust by hand. Nothing is built from it yet. Level 5 means the area is the most likely to be blocked by gangs in the game (and has the fewest police), level 1 the least. The "Your level" column is empty on purpose: fill it in, and the draft level only matters where you leave it alone.

## 1. What the numbers are

No public source publishes crime counts for Palermo below the whole city (the Comune's Repertorio Statistico has city totals only, 34,568 reported crimes in 2023). The only quantified neighbourhood data is ISTAT's IDISE, the socio-economic hardship index. It measures hardship, not crime. It combines nine indicators, with no crime indicator among them: elderly people living alone without an owned home, households with nobody employed or on a work pension, low equivalent income, employment rate 25-64, low work intensity, unstable work, low education 25-64, young people neither working nor studying (NEET, 15-29), and school dropout or repeated years. Each area gets a single score where the Palermo average is 100 and higher means more hardship. ISTAT says the scores are only comparable between areas of the same city in the same year.

Release used: the 2023 release (published 4 December 2025, data updated 21 October 2026), file `082053_Palermo.xlsx` inside `R19_Sicilia_IDISE_2023.zip`. Palermo is published at ASC3 level, the 55 UPL (Unità di Primo Livello) areas. The 2021 Palermo release has the same 55 UPLs; I compared the two and used 2023 as the more recent (see the caveats). The data are residents in households on 31 December 2023, counted in residential census sections of built-up areas, so places without residents are not measured.

Licence: I found no licence statement for the dataset itself, either in the files or on the release page. ISTAT's legal notes page (https://www.istat.it/note-legali/) says that, unless stated otherwise, all content on istat.it is under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), which allows reuse and modification, including commercially, with attribution. If these numbers are ever shown or shipped, credit ISTAT, "Disagio socio-economico a livello sub-comunale (IDISE), anno 2023", and say that levels were derived and adjusted.

Banding: the 55 UPLs are ranked by IDISE and split into quintiles of 11 UPLs each, level 5 being the 11 with the highest hardship. The bands are:

| Level | IDISE range of its 11 UPLs | Rule that reproduces it |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 103.89 to 111.34 | IDISE 103.89 or more |
| 4 | 102.22 to 103.73 | 102.22 up to 103.73 |
| 3 | 99.84 to 102.04 | 99.84 up to 102.04 |
| 2 | 96.99 to 99.62 | 96.99 up to 99.62 |
| 1 | 92.44 to 95.28 | 95.28 or less |

The cuts therefore fall in the gaps 103.73/103.89, 102.04/102.22, 99.62/99.84 and 95.28/96.99. I chose quintiles over natural breaks because they give the five levels the same weight and are easy to explain. The natural-breaks alternative is in the caveats.

How the rest of the table was built:

- Game areas are the 50 names in `QUARTIERI` (src/main.js) plus the 37 zone ids in `ZONES` (src/districts.js). Quartieri are placed by their coordinates. A zone is placed through its seeds in `SEEDS_LL`, and its level is the band that holds more than half of its seeds; if no band does, it needs your rating.
- "Game zone at its point" is what `zoneAt` returns at the quartiere's coordinates with no street name. It is only a hint, see the caveats.
- Drivers are the three of the nine elementary indicators that differ most from the Palermo average (the city row of the ISTAT file), in the direction of the area's own score: the worst three when IDISE is above 100, the best three when it is below. Differences under 5 percent of the city value are dropped. For the employment rate a lower value is the worse one.
- Every value in the table is copied from the ISTAT file. Nothing is averaged or estimated. Where an area spans several UPLs, the table shows the main one and lists the others, it does not blend them.

How game areas were matched to UPLs. The xlsx files have no geometry, so I rebuilt the UPL outlines from the 3,600 census-section polygons embedded in the release's `Palermo.html` map; each section there carries its UPL code. Each game point was then tested against those polygons (point in section). I cross-checked with names: 37 quartieri carry a name that appears in a UPL name (aliases: Albergheria and Albergaria, Zen and San Filippo Neri, Villaggio Santa Rosalia and S.Rosalia, Monte Pellegrino and Montepellegrino), and in all of them the section hit lands in the same UPL. The other 13 are matched by section only, and the table says so. One quartiere point, Pomara, is outside every section; it is 56 m from the nearest one and uses it. For zone seeds the limit was 100 m; three of the 105 seeds were farther (one in `porto`, two in `rural`) and were ignored.

## 4. Notes and caveats

- Hardship is not crime. IDISE tracks unemployment, income, schooling and similar. It ranks the historic centre and the big public-housing estates high, which fits "gangs block the road", but it carries no information about crime that does not follow hardship. Adjust by hand where you know better.
- The range is narrow: the 55 UPLs run from 92.44 to 111.34 around 100. Neighbouring levels 2, 3 and 4 differ by one or two points, and the dataset does not claim that gap is meaningful. Level 5 is the loosest band: it runs from Altarello at 103.89 to San Filippo Neri (ZEN) at 111.34.
- Natural breaks (Jenks, five classes) on the same 55 values would cut at 95.28, 99.94, 102.75 and 106.24, giving a top class of only three UPLs: San Filippo Neri 111.34, Palazzo Reale o Albergaria 109.71 and Monte di Pietà o Seralcadi 107.61 (in the game: Zen, Albergheria, Il Capo). If you want a sharper level 5, that is the cleaner cut. The table follows quintiles as agreed.
- Several game areas share one UPL and so share one value: Roccella, Bandita, Acqua dei Corsari and Pomara (Roccella Acqua dei Corsari); Ciaculli and Croce Verde; Partanna, Mondello, Valdesi and Addaura (Partanna Mondello); Baida and Boccadifalco; Borgo Nuovo and Bellolampo; Cruillas and Inserra; Pagliarelli and Molara (Mezzomonreale); Settecannoli and Romagnolo; Tommaso Natale and Cardillo. Mondello, a beach resort, inherits the level of the whole Partanna Mondello UPL.
- Zisa straddles two UPLs with very different scores: Zisa - Ingastone 105.11 (L5, chosen because the quartiere point is inside it) and Zisa - 4 Camere 97.22 (L2, within 250 m). The same kind of boundary issue is flagged in the matched column for Olivuzza, Passo di Rigano, Quartiere Matteotti and Acquasanta. A quartiere is an OSM point here, not an area, so a point near a boundary can reasonably go either way.
- Bellolampo and Inserra use the UPL of the section their point sits in (Borgo Nuovo and Cruillas) with no name evidence. Monte Pellegrino is matched by name to the UPL Montepellegrino (97.0, 15,552 residents), which is clearly larger than the mountain, so treat that level as low confidence.
- The ten zones that are rated only by a majority of seeds ("mixed bands" in the matched column) are corso, old, kalsa, sacco, resut, zisa, oreto, corsoMille, zen and sferra. The table names every UPL involved, so you can see how thin each majority is.
- 2021 versus 2023: the Palermo 2021 release has the same 55 UPLs. The IDISE rank correlation between the two years is 0.997, 53 of 55 UPLs sit in the same quintile and none moves by two bands or more, so the choice of year barely changes the draft.
- ISTAT's June 2024 report on the 25 official quartieri (`Approfondimento_Palermo.pdf`) is text only, with no table and no IDISE, for a coarser partition and 2021 figures. I used it as a plausibility check only. It names Palazzo Reale-Monte di Pietà, Brancaccio-Ciaculli and Pallavicino as having the highest unemployment and NEET shares. The first two agree with the level 5 above. The report's Pallavicino is a larger area than the UPL of the same name (L2), and the files do not say which UPLs it contains, so I did not use it.
- Design smell worth a separate issue: the game has three different partitions, 50 QUARTIERI points, 37 style zones and 55 UPLs, and none maps cleanly onto another. `zoneAt` at a quartiere point often returns a generic zone (Sferracavallo and Villagrazia give `rural`, Tommaso Natale gives `periph`, Pallavicino gives `favorita`, Partanna gives `tn`), and 19 of the 55 UPLs have no quartiere at all, among them 8 of the 11 level 1 UPLs (the Libertà and Notarbartolo belt), which exist in the game only as zones (`viaLib`, `sacco`, `politeama`). If a danger lookup by position is built later, keying it on UPL (a section-to-UPL grid made from the same Palermo.html polygons) would avoid inheriting those mismatches. I can prepare that grid as a separate task if you want it.
- The zones `avenue` and `calat` are decided by street names in `zoneAt`, not by seeds, so they cannot be placed on an UPL here.
