# Pedestrian sprites (LPC): credits

The people of Palermo Drive are composed from layers of the [Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator) (commit 86160e7507d6841259cff624a51a63c1d4847ee3), whose art comes from the Liberated Pixel Cup and many contributors on OpenGameArt, with our own painted details on top (see LICENSE.md).
Every layer used is listed with its authors, the licences its authors offer it under, and its source. Where a layer is offered under a permissive licence (CC0, CC-BY or OGA-BY) as well as CC-BY-SA / GPL, we use it under the permissive one (shown in the last column); no layer used is share-alike only. All of them require credit, given here and in the game's credits.
