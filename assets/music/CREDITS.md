# Car radio music (src/radio.js, assets/music/)

Four free-licence tracks for the in-game radio, all verified on the source page as CC BY 4.0, CC0 or public domain
(the Alamia e Sperandeo tracks have their own section below).
Every file was re-encoded for the web with ffmpeg: leading silence trimmed, loudness normalised to about -17 LUFS,
resampled to 44.1 kHz and written as MP3 (96 kbps stereo for the modern recordings, 64 kbps mono for the
historical 78 rpm and band recordings). Nothing else was changed (no edits, no cuts at the end).

## Alamia e Sperandeo (32 tracks)

Songs by Alamia e Sperandeo, rights holder Seamusica S.r.l. (https://www.seamusica.shop/). Used in this game with the
permission of the rights holders. Each source URL is the track's own YouTube page on the rights holder's channel,
as written in the comment tag of the file we received.

Changes made to every track: re-encoded for the web (Opus in WebM, about 48 kbps, as `<id>.webm`, and HE-AAC in
M4A, about 40 kbps, as `<id>.m4a`, for browsers that cannot play Opus in WebM), loudness normalised to about -17 LUFS,
leading silence trimmed, metadata reduced to title and artist. Nothing else was changed (no edits, no cuts).
