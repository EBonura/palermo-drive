- **U Barettu** (granita kiosk, Mondello, needs ?world=1): "Il Baretto" was a family-run kiosk gelateria at Piazza Valdesi, corner of Viale Regina Elena, on the Mondello seafront. Opened 1957 by the Schillaci family (gelati, granite and frappe), closed for good in Sept 2021 after a long dispute with Italo-Belga (the company holding the city's maintenance contract for the avenue) over who owned the kiosk; the family reopened elsewhere (Via Torrearsa, then Via Teti 13 in Mondello).
