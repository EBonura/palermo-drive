# Drowning lines to review

Shown on the game-over overlay when the lapa drives into the sea (one picked at random). The card shows the line of the language chosen in Options (the Italian/Palermitan one or the English one), never both.
They live in the `LINES` array in `src/sea.js`; if you change a line here, change it there too (or tell me and I will).
I am not a native Palermitan: anything with Sicilian spelling is a guess and marked (?). Please correct spelling and idiom.

Overlay: title **Tuffo!** (subtitle "Game over - Splash"), buttons **Riprova** (English: Retry; Enter) and **Menu** (Esc).

## Lines

1. Minchia, e unni pensavi d'annari? A Tunisi? (?)  
   _Damn, where did you think you were going? Tunis?_
2. Il Foro Italico finisce lì, genio. Dopo c'è il mare.  
   _The Foro Italico ends there, genius. After that it's the sea._
3. Santa Rosalia fa miracoli, ma non per chi guida così.  
   _Santa Rosalia works miracles, but not for driving like that._
4. Cercavi un arancino, hai trovato il fondale.  
   _You were after an arancino, you found the seabed._
5. A mari ci vai cu 'a barca, non cu 'a lapa! (?)  
   _You go to sea in a boat, not in a lapa!_
6. Nemmeno il tempo di dire "cannolo" e sei già sott'acqua.  
   _Not even time to say "cannolo" and you're already under._
7. Ti serviva una granita, ma questa è salata.  
   _You wanted a granita, but this one is salty._
8. Talia ca bagnu! Fuori stagione, pure. (?)  
   _Look at that swim! Out of season, too._
9. La lapa galleggia come un sasso. Lo sapevi? Ora sì.  
   _The lapa floats like a rock. Did you know? Now you do._
10. Mamma mia, che tuffo. Voto: sei e mezzo.  
    _Mamma mia, what a dive. Score: six and a half._
